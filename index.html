02 / MY JOURNEY LEARNING BY DOING

NOT A STRAIGHT LINE

Every chapter taught me something useful.

My career story didn't begin with a job title. It began with small experiments, unexpected interests, and learning what happens when an idea meets real people.

01
JUNIOR HIGH

🍪 The Cookie Experiment

Learning to sell.

School project → sold out

My first experience with business started with a simple school project: making and selling cookies. When they didn't sell among students, I moved to the school gate and sold them there instead.

It was a small experiment, but it taught me early that selling isn't just about having something good. You have to notice where demand is and be willing to change your approach.

What stayed with me
Customer understanding Direct selling Adaptability
02
SENIOR HIGH

🎭 The Theatre Club

Learning to serve.

Ticketing · Guest experience

Theatre introduced me to another side of business: the experience around the transaction.

Through ticketing and guest-related activities, I learned to interact with people, handle their needs, and understand the experience from the customer's side — not simply the seller's.

What stayed with me
Customer service Communication Experience management
03
ITB

🧃 Making Things Happen

Learning to execute.

~15 canteens · ~98% stock sold

At ITB, my business experiments became more hands-on. I started working with actual products, customers, distribution, and inventory.

One experience involved supplying beverages to around 15 ITB canteens, with approximately 98% of stock sold. It taught me what happens after the idea: coordinating products, managing execution, and paying attention to whether the numbers actually work.

What stayed with me
Operations Project execution Sales management
04
ECOPLAST

🌱 Learning to Influence

Learning to pitch.

Startup · Pitching · Competitions

As Chief Marketing Officer of ECOPLAST, I moved beyond simply executing tasks.

Working in an early-stage startup environment taught me how to communicate a business idea, develop its marketing direction, and represent the startup through pitching and competitions.

I learned that marketing isn't simply about making something attractive. You need to understand the problem, communicate the value clearly, and convince people that the solution matters.

What stayed with me
Marketing communication Pitching Persuasion
05
UNIVERSITY

👥 Building Communities

Learning to gather people.

3 communities · 30K+ combined followers

University gave me opportunities to build communities around shared interests — from student initiatives to digital communities.

These experiences taught me that community building is fundamentally about understanding what people care about, creating reasons for them to participate, and keeping them engaged over time.

What stayed with me
Community building Audience understanding Engagement
06
UNIVERSITY

🍚 The Rice Bowl Experiment

Learning to validate.

Product experiment · Consumer feedback

Another university business experiment involved testing a simple food concept and learning directly from customers.

Instead of assuming what people would buy, I learned to pay attention to actual responses, feedback, and repeat behaviour — a mindset that later became central to my interest in consumer insights.

What stayed with me
Consumer validation Product testing Feedback analysis
07
2021 — 2025

🌏 SellKpopVT

Learning to build at scale.

International digital business

I founded SellKpopVT, an international digital business serving customers across countries and time zones.

Running the business gave me practical experience across customer acquisition, pricing, community management, operations, customer service, and digital marketing.

More importantly, it taught me how customer behaviour changes when people are buying digitally, and how much trust, responsiveness, and community influence purchasing decisions.

What stayed with me
Digital business Customer acquisition Cross-border operations
08
2026

📊 PT Cahaya Segara Indonesia

Learning to support decisions.

Administrative · Executive support

My work with PT Cahaya Segara Indonesia exposed me to the operational side of business from a different angle.

Supporting administrative and executive activities gave me a closer view of how information, organization, and execution support everyday business decisions.

What stayed with me
Organization Executive support Business operations
