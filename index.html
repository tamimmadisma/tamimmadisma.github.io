02 / MY JOURNEY

FROM COOKIES TO COMMUNITIES.

It started with
a box of cookies.

I don't think I discovered business through a textbook. Looking back, the pattern was always there: people, curiosity, and making things happen.

🍪
JUNIOR HIGH

The Cookie Experiment

Learning to sell.

01

My class had a project to create and sell a product. My team made cookies, but nobody wanted to buy them. So I walked to the school gate where parents were waiting and started selling. Eventually, we sold everything.

It was probably my earliest lesson in finding an opportunity and taking action when something wasn't working.

🎭
SENIOR HIGH

The Theatre Club

Learning to serve.

02

I joined a theatre club and handled ticketing rather than performing. I checked tickets, welcomed guests, and helped people find their seats.

A senior later told me they remembered how kind I was and how good the service had been. That stayed with me.

🧃
ITB · TECHNO ENTREPRENEUR CLUB

Making Things Happen

Learning to execute.

~98%

I worked on a fresh juice project supplying around 15 canteens at ITB. I explored Pasar Caringin for cheaper ingredients, helped prepare the products, distributed them early in the morning, and sold what remained directly to students around campus.

We usually managed to sell around 98% of our stock. I loved the entire cycle: finding, making, selling, measuring, adjusting, and trying again.

🌱
ECOPLAST

Learning to Influence

Learning to communicate an idea.

PITCH

Through ECOPLAST, I discovered another side of business: pitching ideas. I represented the startup in competitions and investor-facing situations.

It taught me that communication isn't just about speaking confidently. It's about understanding an idea well enough to make someone else believe in it.

💜
K-POP & COMMUNITY ERA

Building Communities

10K+ × 3

While working on my thesis, I became active in K-pop fandom communities and eventually built communities of my own. Across three fandom accounts, I reached more than 10,000 followers each.

I became fascinated by engagement, trust, content, relationships, and what turns strangers into a community.
