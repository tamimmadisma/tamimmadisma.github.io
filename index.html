02 / MY JOURNEY LEARNING BY DOING

NOT A STRAIGHT LINE

Every chapter taught me something useful.

My career story didn't begin with a job title. It began with small experiments, unexpected interests, and learning what happens when an idea meets real people.

01
JUNIOR HIGH

🍪 The Cookie Experiment

Learning to sell.

School project → sold out

My first experience with business started with a simple school project: making and selling cookies. When they didn't sell among students, I moved to the school gate and sold them there instead.

It was a small experiment, but it taught me early that selling isn't just about having something good. You have to notice where demand is and be willing to change your approach.

What stayed with me
Customer understanding Direct selling Adaptability
02
SENIOR HIGH

🎭 The Theatre Club

Learning to serve.

Ticketing · Guest experience

Theatre introduced me to another side of business: the experience around the transaction.

Through ticketing and guest-related activities, I learned to interact with people, handle their needs, and understand the experience from the customer's side — not simply the seller's.

What stayed with me
Customer service Communication Experience management
03
ITB

🧃 Making Things Happen

Learning to execute.

~15 canteens · ~98% stock sold

At ITB, my business experiments became more hands-on. I started working with actual products, customers, distribution, and inventory.

One experience involved supplying beverages to around 15 ITB canteens, with approximately 98% of stock sold. It taught me what happens after the idea: coordinating products, managing execution, and paying attention to whether the numbers actually work.

What stayed with me
Operations Project execution Sales management
04
ECOPLAST

🌱 Learning to Influence

Learning to pitch.

Startup · Pitching · Competitions

As Chief Marketing Officer of ECOPLAST, I moved beyond simply executing tasks.

Working in an early-stage startup environment taught me how to communicate a business idea, develop its marketing direction, and represent the startup through pitching and competitions.

I learned that marketing isn't simply about making something attractive. You need to understand the problem, communicate the value clearly, and convince people that the solution matters.

What stayed with me
Marketing communication Pitching Persuasion
05
K-POP ERA

💜 Building Communities

Learning what makes people stay.

10K+ followers × 3 communities

I began building online communities around something I genuinely understood as a consumer. Over time, I built and managed three separate K-pop communities, each reaching more than 10,000 followers.

Beyond growing the audience, I learned through experience what people respond to, what makes them engage, and what gives them a reason to come back.

This became an unexpected, hands-on education in audience behavior, content, engagement, community building, and retention.

What stayed with me
Community building Content strategy Audience engagement
06
ICE BSD

🍚 The Rice Bowl Experiment

Learning from the numbers.

25 prepared → 22 sold

At ICE BSD, I put my selling instincts into another real-world test. I prepared 25 portions and sold 22.

The important lesson wasn't simply the sell-through rate. I had to observe the environment, understand customer movement, recognize an opportunity, and make decisions based on what was happening around me.

It reinforced an approach that has continued throughout my journey: observe → test → adapt.

What stayed with me
Market observation Field sales Decision-making
07
SELLKPOPVT

💼 Turning Community Into Business

From audience to revenue.

International customers · Operations

My experience building K-pop communities eventually became a business.

I founded SellKpopVT, turning my understanding of an online audience into a digital business serving customers internationally.

I handled the business end-to-end — including customer acquisition, communication, pricing, fulfillment, operations, and retention — while also developing an operating structure with employees.

What stayed with me
Entrepreneurship Customer acquisition Business operations
08
PT CAHAYA SEGARA

🗂️ Beyond the Admin Title

Learning the business behind the paperwork.

Project-based · Business support

My project-based experience with PT Cahaya Segara gave me exposure to a side of business I hadn't experienced as closely before.

I worked on documentation, reports, proposals, and tender preparation, giving me a closer look at the processes that support commercial projects behind the scenes.

It showed me that business isn't only about the visible customer-facing side. Strong execution also depends on organization, coordination, accuracy, and the systems supporting a project.

What stayed with me
Project coordination Business administration Attention to detail
