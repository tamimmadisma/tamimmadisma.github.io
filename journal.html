TODAY'S NOTE

Maybe “I Can't” Was Too Easy

Today was one of those days where several things seemed to happen at once.

A business partner from the Philippines reached out about an upcoming M COUNTDOWN live voting period. They were looking for around 3,000 votes, while with my current manpower, I could realistically handle around 1,500.

Rather than immediately turning the request down, I decided to see if I could find a way to increase my capacity.

Instead of asking, “Do I have enough people?” I started asking, “Can I find the people I need?”

I started reaching out to potential new employees through Threads. This wasn't easy to do alongside everything else. I was recruiting while simultaneously running my digital business, managing my existing team, handling ongoing work, and making sure everything continued moving.

I reached out to around 20 people.

In the end, only one person was able to handle the programming and workflow required.

It wasn't the number I initially hoped for, but it was still progress.

It also reminded me that building a team isn't simply about finding more people. It's about finding the right people who can actually contribute.

The same day also brought some encouraging developments in my career. An opportunity I had been pursuing moved forward, while another professional opportunity was progressing through its assessment stage.

Having different things develop at the same time made the day feel significant. It was a reminder that the work I've been putting into my business, my skills, and my career is gradually creating new possibilities.

What I learned today

Maybe I don't need to have enough before I begin.

Sometimes, I just need to begin — and figure out what “enough” looks like along the way.

Maybe “I can't” was too easy.
