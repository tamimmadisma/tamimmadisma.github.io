I DON'T REALLY LIKE LEAVING A “WHY?” UNANSWERED.

I naturally want to understand how things work, why people do what they do, and whether there's another way of approaching a problem.


That curiosity tends to show up in how I work: I explore possibilities, question assumptions, connect seemingly unrelated ideas, and learn fastest when I can actually try something.

The catch

CURIOSITY
CAN BE
A DISTRACTION.

Having a lot of ideas is useful — until the next idea becomes more exciting than finishing the current one. Routine work can also feel harder when there's something new to explore.

The challenge for me is not becoming less curious. It's learning which ideas deserve to be finished, then building enough structure to actually finish them.
