04 / The catch
The same curiosity that creates possibilities can also create distractions.

I can become more excited about the next idea than finishing the current one. Routine work can be harder to stay engaged with, and having many possible directions can make commitment feel less exciting than exploration.

So the challenge isn't generating ideas. It's deciding which ones deserve to be finished — then building enough structure to actually finish them.

06 / A work in progress

The useful part isn't the four letters.

01 / CURIOSITY

Keep the curiosity.

I don't want to become less curious, less experimental, or less ambitious. Those are some of the things that make me enjoy building, researching, and trying unfamiliar things.

02 / CONSISTENCY

Build the follow-through.

The next level is turning curiosity into consistency: staying with an idea after the initial excitement fades, creating systems for details I might otherwise overlook, and becoming more intentional about how I communicate.
