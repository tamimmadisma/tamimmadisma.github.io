## About

This repository contains my personal portfolio website, showcasing selected work across business development, marketing, consumer insights, e-commerce, entrepreneurship, research, and commercial projects.

I’m a Business Management graduate from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) with hands-on experience building businesses, growing digital communities, developing marketing strategies, conducting consumer research, and working on commercial projects.

## Why I built this

I wanted to create a digital portfolio that brings my business experience, projects, research, and work samples together in one place.

Rather than relying on a standard portfolio template, I built and continuously developed the website myself, experimenting with its structure, content, design, and functionality along the way.
