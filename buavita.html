01 / The case

One brand.
Eight questions.

What does Buavita want to be — and how is it actually experienced by consumers?

This project examines Buavita from multiple perspectives, combining brand analysis, consumer research, competitive analysis, marketing communications, and netnography.

The research was developed as a series of academic marketing studies, with each article examining a different aspect of the brand and its relationship with consumers.

Together, the studies provide a broader understanding of Buavita's positioning, target market, competitive environment, communication strategy, consumer perceptions, and potential areas for improvement.

04 / Research approach

How the
investigation
unfolded.

Four connected stages moved the project from understanding the brand itself toward identifying strategic issues and potential areas for improvement.

01

Understand the Brand

Brand Inventory

Examined Buavita's brand elements, products, packaging, campaigns, and consumer-facing activities to establish the foundation for the analysis.

02

Understand the Market

Consumers & Competitors

Identified target customer groups and analyzed the competitive landscape to understand Buavita's position within the category.

03

Understand the Consumer

Netnography & Brand Exploratory

Examined online consumer conversations, responses, perceptions, and associations to uncover consumer-level insights.

04

Translate Insights

Strategic Brand Issues

Connected the research findings to Buavita's communication, positioning, and broader strategic challenges to identify potential areas for improvement.
