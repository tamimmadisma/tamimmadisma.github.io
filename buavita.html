Understanding Buavita.

A comprehensive brand research project exploring Buavita's brand identity, consumers, competitors, communications, and strategic issues through qualitative and quantitative research.

BVT
01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A closer look at
the brand.

This project examines Buavita from multiple perspectives, combining brand analysis, consumer research, competitive analysis, marketing communications, and netnography.

The research was developed as a series of academic marketing studies, with each article examining a different aspect of the brand and its relationship with consumers.

Together, the studies provide a broader understanding of Buavita's positioning, target market, competitive environment, communication strategy, consumer perceptions, and potential areas for improvement.

02 / RESEARCH SERIES
01 · BRAND ANALYSIS

Brand Inventory of Buavita ↗

An analysis of Buavita's brand elements, products, packaging, visual identity, campaigns, events, and marketing activities to understand how the brand presents itself to consumers.

Brand Identity · Brand Elements · Marketing Activities
02 · CONSUMER & COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

Who Are Buavita's Goal Customers & Rivals? ↗

Examined Buavita's primary, secondary, and tertiary target customers alongside direct, indirect, and replacement competitors.

Target Market · Competitor Analysis · Consumer Segmentation
03 · BRAND POSITIONING

Points of Parity & Difference ↗

Explored the points of parity and points of difference that shape Buavita's competitive positioning within the packaged fruit juice category.

Brand Positioning · POP · POD · Competitive Advantage
04 · MARKETING COMMUNICATIONS

Brand Communications of Buavita ↗

Analyzed how Buavita communicates its brand through advertising, digital platforms, campaigns, social media, and other consumer touchpoints.

Integrated Marketing · Digital Communication · Campaigns
05 · STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

Brand Issue of Buavita ↗

Synthesized findings from Buavita's brand touchpoints, target market, competitors, communications, and consumer interactions to identify key strategic brand issues and opportunities for improvement.

Strategic Analysis · Consumer Insights · Brand Strategy
06 · NETNOGRAPHY

Buavita's Netnography ↗

Conducted online consumer research by observing and analyzing conversations, comments, and consumer responses across Buavita's digital platforms.

Netnography · Online Consumer Research · Sentiment Analysis
07 · BRAND EXPLORATORY

Buavita's Brand Exploratory ↗

Explored consumer perceptions and associations surrounding Buavita to better understand how the brand is experienced and perceived in the marketplace.

Consumer Perception · Brand Associations · Brand Equity

Qualitative & Quantitative Research ↗

Combined qualitative and quantitative perspectives to examine consumer responses and generate a more comprehensive understanding of Buavita's market and brand position.

Qualitative Research · Quantitative Research · Consumer Insights
