1 · Founder · International D2C on X

SellKpopVT: turning an X audience into 1,000+ orders

Project overview

An international direct-to-consumer business selling K-pop fan-voting services, founded and operated by me end to end. I handled customer research, acquisition, pricing, promotion, sales, customer relationships, operations and team coordination, with customers and resellers across Southeast Asia, Europe and the United States.

The challenge

An online audience had to become paying and repeat customers across different countries, currencies, payment methods and time zones. The business also needed to compete with other voting-service resellers while maintaining customer trust and reliable fulfillment.

My process
  • Studied customer demand through purchasing behaviour, customer questions, responses, market trends and recurring requests.
  • Identified potential customers and promoted the service repeatedly through online communities and digital channels.
  • Handled customer inquiries, order coordination, follow-ups and relationship management throughout the buying process.
  • Set and adjusted product pricing based on customer demand, market conditions and competitor pricing.
  • Ran promotional campaigns and monitored customer responses and sales to understand which approaches generated interest and purchases.
  • Benchmarked 4 competing resellers on pricing, branding, audience size and trust signals to understand the market.
  • Negotiated with resellers and adapted offers rather than treating price objections as automatic lost opportunities.
  • Led 3 core team members, including 2 in Indonesia and 1 in the Philippines, with each member coordinating a small support team.
  • Worked with multiple payment and delivery methods, including PayPal, GCash, QRIS and Wise.
  • Maintained consistency across repetitive daily outreach, marketing, customer service and operational tasks.
Key insight

Direct competitor research showed that the market was not only competing on price. Some sellers focused heavily on being the cheapest, while others competed through audience trust, branding and service reliability. I positioned SellKpopVT around consistency, customer communication, broad payment coverage and visible proof of completed orders rather than relying only on price reductions.

2 · Content · Organic TikTok

Organic TikTok account: 4.25M+ views from 16 posts

Project overview

An unpaid TikTok account I grew as the creator and owner, testing how far organic content could travel without relying on paid promotion.

The challenge

Generating meaningful reach without advertising while working primarily with photo-based content on a video-heavy platform.

My process
  • Published 16 mostly photo-based posts and tested different topics, formats and presentation styles.
  • Tracked views, likes, comments and shares to understand how audiences responded to different posts.
  • Observed which topics and formats generated unusually high reach and used those results to guide subsequent content.
  • Reviewed performance repeatedly rather than treating one viral post as the entire strategy.
Key insight

The account reached more than 4.25 million total views from 16 posts. Across the last 10 posts, the account averaged 369,187 views per post with a 6.42% engagement rate, showing that photo-led content could still generate substantial organic distribution when the topic and presentation matched audience interest.

7 · Business Intern · Corporate training (CIEL, SBM ITB)

CIEL SBM ITB: market research and sizing for a training provider

Project overview

An internship project for CIEL, a corporate training provider at SBM ITB, completed as part of a team of eight across two semesters of the Integrative Business Experience course. The work covered market research, competitor benchmarking, market sizing and a digital response plan during COVID-19.

The challenge

Finding where a corporate training provider could expand beyond its existing market and supporting the opportunity with field research, competitor evidence and market sizing rather than assumptions.

My process
  • Surveyed 16 working professionals about previous training experiences and satisfaction.
  • Observed a live Pertamina R&D training at Patra Hotel Bandung on 16–17 October 2019.
  • Benchmarked three competitors — PM ELI, Binus Mini MBA and MDI — on pricing and service features.
  • Used Porter's Five Forces to analyse the competitive environment.
  • Built a Value Proposition Canvas for teacher digital-transformation training.
  • Used input from teachers and principals to refine the proposed training proposition.
  • Sized the market from schools across Jakarta, West Java and Banten, narrowing the opportunity according to accreditation, digital readiness and ability to pay.
  • When COVID-19 disrupted campus activity, developed a five-week remote social-media mitigation plan for the Business Lab.

8 · Business Development · Management Support

Business Development & Management Support: turning time-sensitive work into deliverables

Project overview

Alongside my own businesses and projects, I supported senior management with practical business-development and commercial work. The role required switching between strategic and detailed tasks, understanding what management needed and turning incomplete information or tight deadlines into usable deliverables.

The challenge

Business-development work often moved quickly. Proposals, tender administration, client reports and financing discussions could arrive at the same time and required accuracy, responsiveness and follow-through.

My process
  • Supported the preparation and development of business proposals for potential clients and opportunities.
  • Handled tender administration and the supporting documentation required for submissions.
  • Prepared and organised client reports so that information could be communicated clearly to management and clients.
  • Supported discussions around business financing by organising information and helping present the relevant business case.
  • Worked directly with senior management on commercial tasks and adjusted priorities as deadlines changed.
  • Followed through on repetitive administrative details as well as higher-level commercial work, rather than focusing only on the more visible parts of a project.
  • Worked independently when necessary while keeping management updated on progress and outstanding requirements.
Key insight

The experience strengthened the practical side of business development: commercial work is not only about generating ideas or speaking with clients. It also depends on being able to execute the documentation, follow-ups, analysis and administrative details that allow an opportunity to move forward.
