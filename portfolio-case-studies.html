Entrepreneurship · D2C · Customer Acquisition

SellKpopVT: turning an X audience into 1,000+ orders

Built and operated an international D2C K-pop fan-voting service, managing customer research, acquisition, pricing, promotion, sales, customer relationships, operations, and team coordination across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the United States.

2021–2025

The business

Building demand around a niche digital service.

I managed SellKpopVT end-to-end, from understanding who was likely to buy and what they valued to setting prices, promoting services, handling sales, maintaining customer relationships, and coordinating day-to-day operations.

The business served customers and resellers across Southeast Asia, Europe, and the United States. Because the service was highly dependent on timing and customer trust, I paid close attention to purchasing behaviour, recurring requests, questions, market trends, and the way customers responded to different offers.

  • Researched customer purchasing behaviour and recurring demand.
  • Identified potential customers through online communities and digital channels.
  • Handled inquiries, order coordination, and customer follow-ups.
  • Set pricing based on demand, market conditions, and competitor positioning.
  • Created promotional campaigns and monitored response and sales.
  • Maintained customer relationships to encourage repeat purchases.

What I learned

Competing on price alone was not enough. Customers also responded to trust, branding, service reliability, communication, payment coverage, and visible proof that orders had been completed successfully.
